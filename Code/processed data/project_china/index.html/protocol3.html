Visualization protocol

Data source

FAOSTAT
  • FAO strives to provide data for all countries, and for all crops and livestock commodities in terms of production (primary and processed), harvested areas, and live and slaughtered animal numbers.
  • This dataset is meant to provide data at the national, regional, and global level, with some minor exceptions. Country and area codes are assigned following the FAO Statistical Standard on country and area codes for statistical use based on the UN M49.
  • Sector coverage: Crops and livestock, primary and selected derived commodities.

Timestamp

1961-2024
